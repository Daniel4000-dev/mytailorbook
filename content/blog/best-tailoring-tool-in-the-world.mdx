## 6. How to Grow and Scale Your Tailoring Business (Tailoring 101)

Whether you are just picking up a pair of shears for the first time or you have been sewing for twenty years, here are the simple, powerful habits that turn a small tailor shop into a wealthy, respected fashion brand:

### Rule 1: Replace Paper with Your Phone Immediately
Paper is fragile. It burns, tears, gets stained, and gets left behind at home. Your phone is with you everywhere. When you save your client measurements in MyStitchBook, your business is backed up for life. Even if you drop your phone in water and buy a new one, you simply log in and all your customer files are right there waiting for you.

### Rule 2: Keep the Promise of the Ready Date
In the fashion industry, trust is currency. If you tell a client their clothes will be ready on Friday at 3:00 PM, let them be pressed, bagged, and hanging by Thursday evening. If you ever anticipate a delay, communicate two days in advance with a kind message. Professionalism will win you ten times more clients than raw talent alone.

### Rule 3: Reconcile Your Money Every Evening
Never mix shop money with personal pocket money. Every evening before closing the shop, check how much cash was paid in, how much arrived by bank transfer or card machine, and what expenses were spent on thread, buttons, or lining. MyStitchBook keeps these daily payment totals balanced so you always know your true profit.

### Rule 4: Show Off Your Best Work Like a Pro
Never send potential clients twenty blurry photos scattered across a WhatsApp chat. Use MyStitchBook's digital catalogue feature to share a clean, elegant style lookbook with your shop branding. When people see your work presented cleanly, they do not argue about price; they pay with respect.
