A New Workflow: How It Feels
Here is what your workflow looks like with MyStitchBook:
1. The Consultation
When a client walks in or sends a message, you no longer scramble. You open their Customer Profile and instantly have their entire history at your fingertips. Their updated Digital Measurements, past orders, and style preferences are securely stored in one place. You never have to ask a client to "remind you of their size" again.
2. The Vision
During the consultation, instead of scrolling through a messy phone gallery to show them past work, you open your curated Style Gallery and Portfolio right within the app. It's clean, professional, and instantly builds trust. You agree on a design and move forward with confidence.
3. The Production
Once the outfit goes into production, the operational magic happens quietly in the background. The order lives on your Tracking Page, giving you a clear bird's-eye view of what is being cut, what is ready for fitting, and what is completed. You lock the fitting date into the integrated Calendar, ensuring you never accidentally double-book yourself. You know exactly what needs your attention today, tomorrow, and next week.
4. The Handover
When the masterpiece is finished, the final touch of professionalism shines through. With a single tap, you generate and send a branded, digital Receipt. The client receives a beautiful, professional invoice that justifies your premium pricing, rather than a hastily scribbled paper receipt.
